Camera behaviour

      
unlockedthe angle carries over from asset to asset; the distance re-fits to
        each model so a 5-unit weapon and a 170-unit body both fill the frame

      
lockedthe view is completely frozen — angle, zoom, pan and orbit centre.
        Best for flipping through colour variants of one mesh. R always gets you back.
